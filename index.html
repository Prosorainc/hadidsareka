About

I build systems that remove repetitive work — and make the remaining work smarter.

My work sits between business operations and applied AI. I start from the real process: the spreadsheets, the documents, the approvals, the handoffs. Then I design the smallest reliable system that removes the friction — often a document pipeline, sometimes an LLM agent, sometimes just good old-fashioned automation.

I care about things that survive contact with reality: offline-first tools, clear audit trails, no silent failures, and software that non-technical people can actually use.
